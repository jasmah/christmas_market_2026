Three timing decisions that matter

Published event hours are separate from suggested visit times. All times below are local: Central Europe uses CET; Scotland uses GMT, one hour behind.

Munich • Tuesday Dec 22

Residenz closes at 8 PM on its final day

Visit the palace courtyard in the early afternoon. Its usual 9 PM close does not apply on Dec 22. Sendlinger Tor also ends Dec 22, with published daily hours until 9 PM.

Residenz operator ↗ · Sendlinger Tor operator ↗

Munich balcony music begins at 5:15 PM Sunday–Thursday. Be in Marienplatz by 5 PM. Rathaus music programme ↗

Prague • Arrival-day decision

The lamplighter needs an early arrival

Dec 23 is the customary last Advent walk, around dusk, not a guaranteed 4:05 PM appointment. Confirm the 2026 arrangements with Prague City Tourism. Aim to finish hotel bag drop by 2:30 PM and reach the Old Town Bridge Tower by 3:45 PM; skip the walk if delayed.

Náměstí Míru is listed through Dec 24; visit Dec 23 to avoid relying on Christmas Eve hours. Prague City Tourism: Peace Square ↗

Vienna • Saturday Dec 26

Visit Maria-Theresien-Platz first

Official listings disagree: the city event page says 4 PM on Dec 26; Vienna Tourism says 7 PM. Plan for the earlier close and reconfirm before travel. Then visit Stephansplatz (7 PM close) and Rathausplatz (10 PM close), all on their final market day.

City of Vienna: Maria-Theresien-Platz ↗ · Vienna Tourism market calendar ↗

If arrival is late, prioritize Rathausplatz; Belvedere, Schönbrunn and Prater offer alternatives on Dec 27.

DAY 1 • SUNDAY, DECEMBER 20, 2026

🇩🇪 Munich arrival & an easy first evening

Ruby Rosi • Bayerstraße 21
Arrival-dependent

Airport, bags and a warm meal

If arriving at Munich Airport, allow roughly 2–3 hours from landing to hotel bag drop for entry formalities, luggage, the S-Bahn and station access. This is an estimate, not a flight connection guarantee. Zone M alone does not cover the airport. Allow 10–15 minutes inside München Hbf; construction can change exits.

3:00–4:30 PM target

Old Town walk; optional tower

Allow 20–25 minutes from Ruby Rosi through Karlsplatz, Neuhauser Straße and Kaufingerstraße to Marienplatz, plus browsing time. Visit the Kripperlmarkt near St. Peter’s and Rindermarkt. Alter Peter is optional: check admission and weather, allow for stairs and queues, and skip it after a late arrival.

5:00–7:30 PM

Marienplatz music, dinner and early rest

Arrive by 5 PM for the published 5:15 PM balcony music. Sunday stalls close at 8 PM. Try Rahmfleck and roasted almonds, then return to the hotel. If the flight is delayed, move Old Town sightseeing to Dec 22. Rathaus music programme ↗

DAY 2 • MONDAY, DECEMBER 21, 2026

🥨 Nuremberg day trip with an optional Munich nightcap

Stay at Ruby Rosi • reserve ICE seats for four
8:30–10:30 AM target

Munich → Nuremberg by ICE

Choose an actual DB departure arriving by about 10:30 AM; allow roughly 1h 05m–1h 20m on faster direct services, plus 20–30 minutes at the departure station. Buy tickets for four adults and add seat reservations. Discount fares may bind you to a train. The Bayern-Ticket is for regional services, not ICE; its Monday validity normally starts at 9 AM and regional travel takes longer. DB journey planner ↗

10:30–11:50 AM

Königstraße, St. Lorenz and Museumsbrücke

Walk north from Nürnberg Hbf toward Hauptmarkt, allowing 20–25 minutes without stops. Photograph Heilig-Geist-Spital from Museumsbrücke. Handwerkerhof shops open at 11 AM, so save shopping there for the return walk rather than waiting for closed shops.

Noon–2:00 PM

Frauenkirche clock and Christkindlesmarkt

Be facing the Frauenkirche clock before noon for the Männleinlaufen. Browse Hauptmarkt and try Drei im Weggla, Elisenlebkuchen and blueberry Glühwein. Elisenlebkuchen is nut-rich but is not automatically gluten-free: ask about flour and wafers. Nuremberg market ↗

2:00–4:30 PM

Sister Cities, indoor break and Kinderweihnacht

Visit Rathausplatz and Schöner Brunnen, then take a seated warm-up break before Hans-Sachs-Platz. Enjoy the carousel lights; the horse-drawn post coach has a separate route and schedule, so do not assume it departs from Kinderweihnacht. Skip smaller stops if crowds are heavy.

4:30–6:00 PM

Fleischbrücke → Handwerkerhof → station

Optional Feuerzangenbowle near Fleischbrücke, then allow 20–25 minutes back via Handwerkerhof. Its shops normally open Monday–Saturday 11 AM–6 PM; visit around 5 PM. Reach your train platform 20 minutes before departure. Handwerkerhof shop hours ↗

6:00–9:30 PM target

Return train; Tollwood only if everyone has energy

Book a return around 6–7 PM, subject to the timetable. After returning and eating, Tollwood is optional: allow 20–30 minutes to its festival entrance, or U4/U5 one stop to Theresienwiese plus walking. Monday market and food stalls close at 10:30 PM. Tollwood visitor information ↗

DAY 3 • TUESDAY, DECEMBER 22, 2026

🇩🇪 Munich courtyards, food market & final-day visits

Residenz final close: 8 PM • Sendlinger Tor: 9 PM
10:00 AM–noon

Viktualienmarkt breakfast and Old Town

The regular food stalls generally close Sundays, so use Tuesday for cheeses, pastries and food shopping. Individual stalls and winter events set their own hours. Take a seated lunch and warm-up break before the afternoon markets.

1:00–2:30 PM

Residenz courtyard first

Allow 10–15 minutes from Marienplatz to the Kaiserhof entrance. Visit well before its special final-day 8 PM close. Ask each vendor about mug deposits and souvenirs; no universal final-night mug giveaway or sale is promised. Residenz operator ↗

2:45–4:30 PM

Wittelsbacherplatz medieval market

Walk about 5–10 minutes from the courtyard entrance. Try Drachenblut or a non-alcoholic punch and warm food. Leave time for a café stop and a 10–15-minute return to Marienplatz if you want balcony music.

5:00–7:30 PM

Optional 5:15 PM music; choose one last market

After music, choose Sendlinger Tor (allow 15–20 minutes) or Isartor (15–20 minutes). Avoid squeezing in both. Sendlinger Tor ends tonight at 9 PM. Return early enough to pack for Prague and check tomorrow’s departure terminal. Sendlinger Tor operator ↗

DAY 4 • WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 23, 2026

🇨🇿 Munich → Prague; lamplighter if arrival permits

Almanac X Alcron • Štěpánská 40
Early morning target

Book the transfer around the arrival deadline

Plan roughly 5½–6 hours by direct rail or 5–6 hours by coach, depending on the selected service; check the actual Dec 23 timetable and allow delay margin. Aim to arrive in Prague by 1:30 PM to finish bag drop by 2:30 PM. If no suitable early service is available, drop the lamplighter from the plan. Verify whether a coach leaves from Munich ZOB or another stop. DB journey planner ↗ · České dráhy journey planner ↗

Arrival–2:30 PM target

Terminal-specific hotel transfer and lunch

From Florenc: Metro C toward Háje, two stops to Muzeum via Hlavní nádraží, then walk 10–15 minutes. From Praha hlavní nádraží: Metro C one stop toward Háje to Muzeum, or a 15–25-minute walk with bags. A taxi can be simpler for four. Allow 45–60 minutes for terminal access, transit and bag drop.

3:10–4:45 PM target

Charles Bridge and the optional Advent lamplighter

Allow 25–35 minutes from the hotel to the Old Town Bridge Tower at Křižovnické náměstí; be there by 3:45 PM. The customary Dec 23 dusk walk is an experience to confirm with Prague City Tourism, not a guaranteed timed booking. If missed, enjoy the illuminated bridge and Old Town instead.

5:30–7:00 PM

Náměstí Míru before the holiday

From the bridge allow 35–45 minutes: walk to Staroměstská and take Metro A toward Depo Hostivař to Náměstí Míru, three stops via Můstek and Muzeum. The city lists the market 10 AM–8 PM through Dec 24; compare crafts and prices rather than assuming a fixed discount. From the hotel alone allow 15–20 minutes on foot. Prague City Tourism: Peace Square ↗

7:30 PM onward

Dinner near the hotel; tomorrow’s supplies

Wenceslas Square is a short walk from the hotel. Have dinner, buy breakfast/snacks before the holiday closures, and confirm Dec 24 and Dec 25 restaurant reservations. The operator publishes standard 10 AM–10 PM hours; exact holiday exceptions and performance times need reconfirmation. Prague market operator ↗

DAY 5 • THURSDAY, DECEMBER 24, 2026

🇨🇿 Christmas Eve: central markets, river walk & reserved dinner

Holiday hours must be checked • book dinner for four
10:00 AM–noon

Central markets early

Visit Wenceslas Square and Old Town Square while stalls are more likely to be operating. Allow 15–20 minutes between the hotel and Old Town Square. Do not depend on all-day Christmas Eve opening or midnight food stalls: check holiday exceptions with the operator. Prague market operator ↗

Noon–2:00 PM

Lunch, supplies and a short riverside walk

Buy essentials by late morning on Dec 23 if possible; large Czech shops must close at noon on Dec 24, with holiday closures Dec 25–26 and exceptions for certain outlets. Have a confirmed lunch option, then a short Charles Bridge/Kampa stroll if weather permits. Treat any Kampa stalls as a bonus, not a confirmed market.

Afternoon

Hotel rest or a short Old Town outing

Take a warm break. The official Prague Castle Christmas Stroll/labyrinth is closed Dec 24; no Castle Christmas market is confirmed in this guide. Save the optional Castle visit for Dec 25. Check PID Lítačka for holiday service; do not assume normal tram frequencies. Prague Castle Christmas Stroll ↗

6:00–8:00 PM target

Prebooked Christmas Eve dinner

Reserve for four now, confirm the menu, price, last seating and cancellation terms, and keep the hotel restaurant as a backup only if it confirms service. A tree light show or Czech Christmas Mass can be added if the 2026 programme confirms it; neither a 4:30 PM show nor a 9 PM concert is a firm appointment here.

DAY 6 • FRIDAY, DECEMBER 25, 2026

🇨🇿 Optional Castle visit, Lesser Town & relaxed Christmas Day

Choose the Castle route OR a gentler Old Town day
10:00 AM–12:30 PM

Optional Prague Castle Christmas Stroll

If holiday opening is confirmed, allow 40–50 minutes from the hotel: Metro A from Muzeum to Malostranská, then tram 22 uphill to Pražský hrad. The Christmas Stroll/labyrinth lists daily 10 AM–6 PM around this date, with Dec 24 closed; individual Castle interiors have separate hours and admission. Confirm before setting out. Prague Castle Christmas Stroll ↗

12:30–3:00 PM

Reserved lunch and a downhill route

Allow 30–45 minutes from the Castle through Malá Strana toward Charles Bridge, longer with stops. Use transit or a taxi if paths are icy. Reserve Christmas Day lunch; cafés and Havel’s stalls are optional only when open. On a low-energy day, skip the Castle and enjoy Old Town instead.

3:00–6:00 PM

Old Town or Wenceslas Square, then an early dinner

Check that day’s market hours, take photos and try bramboráky or svařák. For ham sold by weight, ask for the price per 100 g, specify the portion and confirm the total. Pack afterward for Vienna; confirm tomorrow’s train and food provision. Allow a café or hotel warm-up every 60–90 minutes outdoors.

DAY 7 • SATURDAY, DECEMBER 26, 2026

🇦🇹 Prague → Vienna; earliest-closing market first

Novotel Wien Hauptbahnhof • Canettistraße 6
Morning → 1:00 PM target

Direct train and a realistic bag-drop buffer

Select a direct Praha hlavní nádraží → Wien Hbf train arriving ideally by 1 PM; allow roughly 4–4½ hours on the train and reserve four seats. Bring breakfast/snacks and reach Prague’s platform 30 minutes early. Allow 45–60 minutes at Vienna for leaving the platform, finding the hotel, bag drop and returning to the U1. Hotel rooms may not yet be ready. České dráhy journey planner ↗ · ÖBB journey planner ↗

2:00–3:00 PM target

Maria-Theresien-Platz FIRST

Take U1 from Südtiroler Platz–Hauptbahnhof toward Leopoldau to Karlsplatz (two stops), then U2 toward Seestadt to MuseumsQuartier (one stop), followed by a short walk. Allow 30–40 minutes from the hotel. Visit by 3 PM and leave before the conservative 4 PM cutoff because official listings conflict (4 PM versus 7 PM). City of Vienna: Maria-Theresien-Platz ↗

3:00–5:30 PM

Hofburg, Graben and Stephansplatz

Walk 25–35 minutes through the Hofburg and Graben to St. Stephen’s Cathedral, allowing extra photo time. Browse Stephansplatz well before its published 7 PM close and take a seated meal or café break. If it is already after 5:30 PM on reaching the center, choose either a short cathedral visit or Rathausplatz rather than rushing both.

6:00–8:30 PM target

Rathausplatz final market evening

Allow 25–35 minutes on foot from Stephansplatz, or take U3 to Volkstheater and U2 to Rathaus plus access time. Enjoy the Tree of Hearts, Käsekrainer and chestnuts; stalls close at 10 PM. If the train is late, skip Maria-Theresien-Platz and prioritize this market. Belvedere is a fallback if you prefer a shorter evening. Vienna Tourism market calendar ↗

DAY 8 • SUNDAY, DECEMBER 27, 2026

🇦🇹 Belvedere & Schönbrunn; Prater is optional

Two core stops • choose only one museum interior
10:00 AM–noon

Belvedere market and optional timed gallery

Allow 15–20 minutes from Novotel to the market entrance at Upper Belvedere. Vienna Tourism lists Sunday market hours 10 AM–9 PM, not a 5 PM close. If seeing Klimt’s The Kiss, reserve a timed Upper Belvedere ticket and allow 60–90 minutes; this may push the next stop later. Vienna Tourism market calendar ↗

Noon–1:30 PM

Lunch and transfer to Schönbrunn

Allow 45–60 minutes door to door: walk or tram D toward the center, connect to U4 at Karlsplatz toward Hütteldorf, exit Schönbrunn and walk to the forecourt. Check live directions for a quicker connection. Have a seated lunch; choose either the Upper Belvedere interior or a Schönbrunn palace tour today.

1:30–5:30 PM target

Schönbrunn market; optional daylight hill walk

The market is published open until 7 PM after Christmas. If footing is safe, use the early afternoon for a hill viewpoint and return before dark. The Gloriette rooftop terrace is seasonally closed; do not plan rooftop access. Park gates close at 5:30 PM in November–December, separately from the market. On ice or in heavy rain, skip the hill. Schönbrunn park and terrace hours ↗

6:15–8:30 PM optional

Prater or an early hotel evening

Leave Schönbrunn around 5:30 PM and allow 45–60 minutes to Riesenradplatz: U4 to Karlsplatz, U1 toward Leopoldau to Praterstern, then walk. Prater’s market is published open until 10 PM; check the day’s music programme and the Ferris wheel’s separate operating hours and tickets. Skip Prater if tired and pack for tomorrow’s flight.

For midnight Hogmanay, confirm an Edinburgh hotel for the night of Dec 31 into Jan 1 and an onward departure after the celebration. If your return flight is Dec 31, skip midnight events. Expert Christmas Market Tips, Tricks & Local Phrases

How to navigate peak evening crowds, get clean souvenir mugs, stay warm on frozen cobblestones, and speak like a local.

🍻 1. The "Table Scout" System for 4 Adults

Standing high-top tables (Stehtische) around Glühwein huts fill up fast between 5:00 PM and 8:00 PM. Split your group of 4 into two pairs: have two people claim half a standing table while the other two buy the four drinks and sausages. Sharing tables with locals is standard etiquette!

🇩🇪 "Entschuldigung, ist hier noch frei?" (Ent-SHOOL-dee-goong, ist heer nokh fry?) — "Excuse me, is this spot free?"

☕ 2. Ask about a clean souvenir mug

Keep any deposit token (Pfandmarke) you receive and ask where mugs can be returned. Rules and deposits vary. Ask permission before keeping a souvenir mug and whether a clean replacement is available; a boxed swap is not guaranteed.

🇩🇪 "Können wir eine frische Tasse zum Mitnehmen haben?" "Can we trade this used mug for a clean one to take home?"

🥾 3. Warm boots, layers and regular indoor breaks

Standing on freezing cobblestones for 4–6 hours drains body heat straight through thin rubber sneaker soles. Wear waterproof boots with thick merino wool socks (or slip adhesive toe-warmers inside your boots), and wear breathable layers you can unzip on heated U-Bahn trains.

🧤 Pocket Napkin & Wet-Wipe Trick: Carry a small pack of wet wipes—candied almonds, powdered sugar, and mustard on wool gloves are an everyday hazard!

🗣️ 4. Quick German & Czech Ordering Phrases

🇩🇪 "Vier Glühwein, bitte!" / "Drei im Weggla, bitte!" "Four mulled wines, please!" / "Three Nuremberg sausages in a roll, please!"
🇩🇪 "Zahlen, bitte — zusammen / getrennt" "The bill, please — together / separate"
🇨🇿 "Dobrý den! Čtyři svařáky, prosím. Děkuji!" (DOB-ree den! CHTIH-zhee SVAH-zhah-kih, PRO-seem. DYEH-koo-yi!) — "Hello! Four mulled wines, please. Thank you!"

📋 5. Book meals, transport and essential tickets

Reserve four seats on intercity trains, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day meals, any museum interiors and ticketed Hogmanay events. Verify passports and entry requirements for each traveller’s nationality, including the UK’s visa/ETA rules where applicable, using official guidance. Keep booking references offline. UK entry checker ↗

🧭 6. Make winter days comfortable

Plan a seated warm-up every 60–90 minutes, choose a meeting point if the group splits, carry a power bank and save hotel addresses offline. Cobblestones, steep paths and tower stairs may be difficult in snow or with limited mobility: choose level routes, lifts or taxis, and skip optional hill climbs. Check toilets and accessible entrances before a long outing.

🎫 7. Activate transit tickets before boarding

Use the official MVV, PID Lítačka and WienMobil apps. Validate paper tickets where required and check activation delays for mobile tickets. A Munich group ticket requires the group to travel together; Vienna tickets are per person. Airport journeys may need extra zones or separate tickets. MVV group day ticket ↗ · Prague public transport fares ↗ · Wiener Linien tickets ↗

💶 8. Tips and food ordering

Check for service charges. If service was good, rounding up a café bill or leaving a modest restaurant tip is a choice, not a required percentage at market stalls. Confirm portion weights and total prices before ordering. Ask about nuts, gluten and dairy; holiday foods and mulled drinks vary by recipe. Non-alcoholic Kinderpunsch is an easy alternative between tasting stops.

🚇 Prague tickets: when singles beat a pass

Current 2026 full adult fares in PID Lítačka are 36 CZK / 30 minutes, 46 CZK / 90 minutes, 140 CZK / 24 hours and 340 CZK / 72 hours; paper equivalents are 39, 50, 150 and 350 CZK. Check age-based eligibility and luggage rules before buying. Tickets are per person; buy enough for all four.

For this mostly walkable stay, two 90-minute rides cost 92 CZK per adult, below a 140 CZK day pass; four such rides cost 184 CZK, so a day pass can make sense on a transit-heavy Castle day. A 72-hour app pass is 1,360 CZK for four full-fare adults, but only worthwhile if the actual rides and expiry fit your plans. It may expire before Saturday’s station transfer if activated early Wednesday. Keep the ticket valid for the entire trip, including transfers.

Activate the app ticket early enough that it shows as valid before entering the metro’s paid area or boarding. Stamp an unvalidated paper ticket once at first use. Official 2026 tariff ↗ · Validity, luggage and concessions ↗
